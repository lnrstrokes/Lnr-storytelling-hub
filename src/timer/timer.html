          YouTube Live Policy Safeguards

          Active Protection
          • Non-templated broadcasts: Storytelling rotates 5 investigative archetypes, shifting visual modes, palettes, and pacing profiles with live deduction gates + authored host commentary so streams avoid “inauthentic/repetitious” flags.
          • Quiz + Story velocity: Optional 2-question rapid trivia bursts between cases pair with 1,800+ non-repeating quiz items and 1,500-item session memory.
          • Copyright-safe audio: The shipped Uplift show bed is an original, royalty-free track generated for this channel, so it is safe to run for hours without a Content ID claim. The procedural pads are synthesised on-device. Only upload custom MP3 tracks you personally hold broadcast rights to.
          • Honest disclosures & 12h VOD: If airing paid sponsor breaks, check “My video contains paid promotion” in YouTube Studio, and restart streams under 12 hours to preserve full VOD archives.
          • Before you go live: set the stream to Not made for kids unless it genuinely is, keep the on-screen reading legible at 1080p, and confirm your capture window is exactly 16:9 so nothing is cropped by YouTube’s player.
