# LNR Storytelling Hub — Social Media Outreach Kit

**Goal:** get creators to open lnr Studio, start a broadcast, and come back tomorrow.
**Not** the goal: impressions, follower counts, or "going viral". One person who runs a three-hour stream tonight is worth more than ten thousand people who scroll past.

Everything below is written to be pasted, not rewritten. Copy blocks are separated by `---`. Replace `[CHANNEL]` and `[LINK]` and publish.

## 17. Guardrails

These are non-negotiable, and they are the reason the copy above is written the way it is.

1. **No income claims.** Never "make $X", "passive income", "monetise in 30 days", or any figure besides the $19 Pro price.
2. **No implication of YouTube partnership or approval.** Say what the app does; never "YouTube-approved" or "YouTube-endorsed".
3. **Disclose sponsorship.** If a post is paid or contains a sponsor break, say so, and tick "Includes paid promotion" on any upload that carries one.
4. **No fake screenshots, dashboards, testimonials or subscriber counts.** Record the real app or show nothing.
5. **One link per post.** No link stacks, no cloaked shorteners.
6. **Respect the room.** Read each community's rules before posting; no unsolicited DM volume; no reposting the same text across servers.
7. **Never quote a limit the app doesn't print.** Free and Pro specifics change; the app's onboarding card is the source of truth.

## 18. Measurement — and the decision rules

Track only these four. Everything else is noise.

| Metric | Where | What it tells you |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sessions by `utm_source` | GA4 (acquisition) | Which platform actually sent people |
| `pwa_install` events | GA4 (already emitted on `appinstalled`) | Which post produced installs, not just visits |
| Trial starts / first broadcast | Your own count of new Pro keys | Which post produced users |
| Pro checkouts | Selar dashboard | Which post produced revenue |

Decision rules, applied every Sunday:

- A platform that sends sessions but **no installs** after 3 posts: change the asset, not the platform.
- A post with installs but **no broadcasts**: the post promised the wrong thing. Fix the promise, not the traffic.
- Best-performing format gets **one more** variant next week. Not five.
- Anything under 200 views across two attempts gets deleted from the calendar, not "boosted".
